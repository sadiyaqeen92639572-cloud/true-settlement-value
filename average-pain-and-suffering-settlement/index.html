Average Pain and Suffering Settlement

Pain and suffering settlements are usually expressed as a multiplier of your economic damages (medical bills plus lost wages), typically 1.5x for minor injuries up to 5x for severe, permanent injuries. There's no fixed dollar 'average' that applies across injury types, since the multiplier is designed to scale with severity rather than produce a flat number.
