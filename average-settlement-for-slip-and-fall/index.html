What Determines a Slip and Fall Settlement

Slip and fall settlements vary more than car accident settlements for a structural reason: you have to prove the property owner's negligence, not just that you were injured. A slip and fall with clear negligence (a known, unaddressed hazard) and serious injury can settle well; a similar injury with disputed negligence often settles for far less, or not at all.
