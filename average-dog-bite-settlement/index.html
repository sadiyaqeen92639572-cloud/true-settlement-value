Average Dog Bite Settlement

Dog bite settlements skew higher than many injury types of comparable medical cost because of scarring and disfigurement, which juries and insurers weigh heavily in the pain-and-suffering multiplier — especially for facial or hand injuries. Most dog bite claims are paid through the owner's homeowners or renters insurance liability coverage.
