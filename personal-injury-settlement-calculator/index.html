Personal Injury Settlement Calculator

Personal injury settlements generally cover two categories: economic damages (medical bills, lost income, property damage) and non-economic damages (pain and suffering). This calculator applies the multiplier method — the same approach insurance adjusters use as a negotiation starting point — to any type of injury claim.

How It's Calculated

This tool uses the multiplier method: (medical bills + lost wages) × a pain-and-suffering multiplier based on injury severity (1.5x for minor, 3x for moderate, 5x for severe), minus any reduction for your percentage of fault. It's the same starting-point approach insurance adjusters commonly use in negotiations — not a guaranteed outcome.

Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as economic damages in a personal injury claim?

Medical bills (past and future), lost wages, lost earning capacity, and property damage — losses with a specific dollar figure attached.

What's the difference between economic and non-economic damages?

Economic damages have a receipt or pay stub behind them. Non-economic damages (pain and suffering, emotional distress, loss of enjoyment of life) don't — they're estimated using methods like the multiplier.

Do I need a lawyer to negotiate a personal injury settlement?

Not always for minor claims, but insurers typically offer less to unrepresented claimants. For serious injuries or disputed liability, legal representation usually improves outcomes.
