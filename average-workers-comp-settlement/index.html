Average Workers' Comp Settlement

Workers' comp settlements are calculated differently from injury lawsuits — there's no pain-and-suffering multiplier. Instead, the amount is driven mainly by your average weekly wage, your state's compensation rate, and your permanent disability rating as determined by a medical evaluation.
