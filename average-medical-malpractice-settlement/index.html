Average Medical Malpractice Settlement

Medical malpractice settlements tend to run higher than most other injury claims because the underlying harm is often severe (surgical errors, misdiagnosis, birth injuries), but many states cap the non-economic (pain and suffering) portion specifically for malpractice cases, which can significantly reduce the total compared to what a similar injury would recover in a standard negligence claim.
