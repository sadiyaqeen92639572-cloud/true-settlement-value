Average Car Accident Settlement Amount

Settlement amounts scale closely with injury severity and treatment duration. Minor soft-tissue injuries with a few weeks of treatment settle far lower than injuries requiring surgery, hospitalization, or ongoing care. Policy limits are the practical ceiling in most cases — a driver with only state-minimum liability coverage caps what you can recover from them directly, regardless of how severe your injuries are.
